## Sub-activities as described in project records

**Hydro-morphology for bridges and rivers**
- Hydrological data collection and analysis.
- Bathymetric survey.
- Hydraulic modelling of river flows.
- Morphological assessment of bank stability and erosion.
- Sediment transport modelling (siltation and scour risk).
- Scour prediction at piers and abutments.
- Bridge pier setting.
- River training works: necessity assessment, design and cost estimation.
- Flood protection structure design.

**Water, sewer and drainage**
- Site and utility assessment.
- Hydrological and hydraulic modelling of water distribution, drainage and sewer networks.
- Design of water supply (source to distribution), sewerage and stormwater drainage.
- Design of water and wastewater treatment plants.
- Drawings, specifications, BOQ and cost estimates.

**Feasibility (treatment plants)**
- Water demand projection and source assessment.
- Water quality data.
- Preliminary layout.
- Intake, transmission and distribution hydraulics.
- EIA and mitigation.
- Technical and economic feasibility.

**Environment and biodiversity**
- Terrestrial and aquatic biodiversity baseline.
- Sensitive species, habitats and hotspots.
- Ambient noise measurement and impact prediction.
- Mitigation and management plans.
- Reports per national regulations.

**Socio-economic and climate studies**
- Ecological and socio-economic baseline assessment.
- Household surveys, FGDs and KIIs with digital instruments.
- Climate and hazard risk assessment.
- Indicator frameworks.
- Panel census design.

**River survey and data** (JICA/BWDB project)
- Baseline and socio-economic study.
- River migration analysis.
- Drone survey.
- Multibeam echo sounder, side-scan sonar and sub-bottom profiler surveys.
- FGD and questionnaire surveys.
- Stakeholder meetings.
- Development of a database system.

**Weather modelling**
- Acquisition and downscaling of global weather data (GFS, ECMWF) using the WRF model.

**Software / GIS** (see [digital-case-studies.md](digital-case-studies.md))
- Web GIS systems.
- Asset management system requirements and configuration.
- AI flood detection from SAR imagery.
- Drone-image processing SaaS.
- Route optimisation SaaS.
