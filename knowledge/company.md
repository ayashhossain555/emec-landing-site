## Profile summary

- An engineering consulting firm working in infrastructure development and environmental management, with public- and private-sector clients.
- **Principal focus:** fluvial geomorphology; hydrologic modelling; 1D/2D/3D hydraulic and morphologic modelling; computational fluid dynamics; artificial intelligence; water resources engineering; municipal infrastructure design.
- **Services offered:** planning, feasibility analysis, public consultation, detail design, contract administration. The profile says it places strong emphasis on environmental considerations.
- It offers specialised hydromorphology and CFD modelling for bridge, river embankment and revetment design problems.
- **Approach:** "multidisciplinary, adaptive management approach"; solutions that are "cost-effective and environmentally sound".
- **Disciplines claimed for ecosystem-based studies:** hydrogeology, aquatic biologists, fluvial geomorphologists, hydrologists, water quality specialists.
- **Commitments:** quality assurance and "standard-keeping backup support of all data and information".
- **Background:** the founders "have a combined experience of 40 years in civil engineering and related fields". The individual figures listed for key staff (30, 31 and 25 years for the top three) add up to far more than 40, which the profile does not explain.
- The LIC portfolio (Sept 2026) describes EMEC as "an engineering consultancy with an in-house software and GIS division that designs and builds web-based geospatial information systems on an open-source stack".
