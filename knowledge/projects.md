## Project narratives

### 1. JICA/BWDB comprehensive river management (Nodi project)
- **Aim:** develop systematic river management methods suited to Bangladesh's diverse rivers, covering the Jamuna and smaller south-eastern rivers (Sangu, Matamuhuri). The project also builds BWDB staff capacity through knowledge tools and management plans.
- **Problems addressed:** bank erosion, sedimentation and channel shifting, in line with Delta Plan 2100 and the SDGs.
- **EMEC services:**
  - Baseline and socio-economic study.
  - River migration analysis.
  - Hydrological data collection.
  - Drone survey.
  - Multibeam echo sounder, side-scan sonar and sub-bottom profiler surveys.
  - FGD/questionnaire surveys and stakeholder meetings.
  - Development of a database system.
- P adds: a web GIS system for BWDB's revetment and embankment monitoring, an environmental (including biodiversity) assessment, and a hydro-morphological assessment.
- **Role (L):** sub-consultant to Oriental Consultants Global and Pacific Consultants. The web GIS is described in [digital-case-studies.md](digital-case-studies.md).

_Source: PDS V2.docx; EMEC Profile.docx; EMEC_Relevant_Portfolio_LIC_Dashboard.pdf_

### 2. Buriganga Bridge, Postagola / Fatulla
- **Scope:** detailed design of a new 4-lane bridge at Postagola and preliminary design of another at Fatulla, to improve Dhaka–Narayanganj connectivity.
- **EMEC services:** hydrological data, hydraulic and morphological analysis, sediment transport modelling, and river training work assessment, design and cost estimation.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 3. Pabna, Kushtia, Narail and Chandpur Economic Zones
- **Scope:** studies of river behaviour, flood risk, erosion and sediment dynamics for the proposed EZs.
- **EMEC services:** bathymetric survey, hydraulic modelling, bank stability assessment, sediment transport modelling, and design and cost estimation of river training and flood protection works.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 4. Cumilla University utilities
- **Scope:** planning, modelling and design of the complete water supply, sewerage and stormwater drainage systems, with hydrological and hydraulic modelling and design of water and wastewater treatment facilities.
- **Outputs:** drawings, specifications and cost estimates.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 5. 41 large bridges (LGED)
- **Scope:** hydrological and morphological studies for 41 proposed bridge sites over 100 m span across Bangladesh, covering flooding, sediment transport, scour, erosion and bridge pier setting.
- **EMEC services:** bathymetric survey, hydraulic modelling, and river training design and costing.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 11. Kewatkhali Bridge feasibility and design
- **Scope:** a comprehensive hydro-morphological study for the Kewatkhali Bridge over the Brahmaputra, with railway overpass and 4-lane approach.
- **EMEC services:** hydrological data, bathymetric survey near the alignment, hydraulic modelling for design parameters, river stability and erosion assessment, prediction of scour at piers and abutments, and recommendations for river training and bank protection.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 19. Sirajganj Economic Zone utilities
- **Scope:** planning, modelling and design of the water supply, sewerage and stormwater drainage systems, plus water and wastewater treatment design, BOQ and cost estimation.
- The datasheet narrative repeats the Cumilla text, including the phrase "university campus".

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 22. Narayanganj 500 MLD surface water treatment plant
- **Scope:** technical, environmental and economic feasibility of a 500 MLD plant, to meet potable demand and reduce groundwater dependency.
- **EMEC services:** demand projection, source assessment, water quality data, preliminary layout, intake/transmission/distribution hydraulics, EIA and cost estimates.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 33. RISe baseline, Barishal (BRAC, RFP BPD/2025/RFP-2826)
- **EMEC role:** lead consultant.
- **Work covered:**
  - Household survey across four low-income informal settlements, with FGDs and KIIs using a structured digital instrument.
  - Settlement mapping by drone survey, RTK ground control and GIS.
  - Assessment of water supply, sanitation and infrastructure.
  - An indicator framework reporting the number and percentage meeting criteria, with no weighting or composite scoring at the client's direction.
  - Hazard and climate risk assessment, including a highest flood level for resilient design, and a framework for midterm and endline re-measurement.

_Source: EMEC_Relevant_Portfolio_LIC_Dashboard.pdf; EMEC Profile.docx_

### 34. CONNECT baseline panel census (GIZ)
- **EMEC role:** implementer, engaged directly by GIZ, for the baseline screener census and panel establishment.
- **Panel:** a verified panel of 3,100 households built through a two-stage instrument (an eligibility gate, then an additive vulnerability scorecard), tracked at baseline, midline and endline.
- **Data protection:** personal identifiers are held separately in a restricted-access register.
- **Locations:** Nijhum Dwip, Naf Peninsula, Saint Martin's.
- P describes the scope as ecological and socio-economic baseline assessment.

_Source: EMEC_Relevant_Portfolio_LIC_Dashboard.pdf; EMEC Profile.docx_

### 36. Mirsarai EZ stormwater drainage (BEZA)
- **Scope:** modelling, detailed design and BOQ for the stormwater drainage of Mirsarai EZ, to manage runoff and minimise flood risk.
- **EMEC services:** site and rainfall data, hydrological and hydraulic modelling, network design, drawings and specifications, BOQ, and recommendations on sustainable drainage.

_Source: PDS V2.docx_

### 38. Kewatkhali Bridge biodiversity and noise assessment (RHD; AIIB per P)
- **Scope:** terrestrial and aquatic biodiversity baseline; sensitive species, habitats and hotspots; ambient noise measurement and impact prediction for the construction and operation phases.
- **Outputs:** mitigation and management plans; reports per national regulations. P describes it as a "biodiversity survey and ... biodiversity monitoring and management plan".

_Source: PDS V2.docx; EMEC Profile.docx_
